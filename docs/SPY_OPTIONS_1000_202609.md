# $1,000 aggressive SPY CALL/PUT month

**September 1–30, 2026: historical research simulation, not a live brokerage return.**

Baseline modeled outcome: **$1,000 → $105.80**, a **$894.20 loss (-89.42%)**. 6 closed trades: 2 wins and 4 losses. No open positions and no broker orders.

## Rules fixed before viewing option outcomes

- One fake creator alert at 09:45 ET for each of 21 sessions. CALL if the first 15 minutes close above the opening price; otherwise PUT.
- Nearest whole-dollar SPY strike and same-day expiry. No hindsight selection of winning strikes or dates.
- Run the real production text parser and one-minute candle gate. Ten alerts confirm; eleven invalidate.
- Allocate up to the full current simulated cash balance to whole contracts; reserve both sides of assumed fees. Residual cash stays unspent when another full contract will not fit.
- Underlying target at 0.5% in the alert direction; stop 0.3% against it. Premium stop at -50%, trailing arms at +30% and exits after a 20-percentage-point giveback. These match the current premium guardrail defaults; execution remains an independent model.
- Observe only completed minute prices. Exit at the next available minute opening proxy, or force out 30 minutes before the scheduled session close. A stop threshold does not guarantee a fill at that threshold.

## Prices, fills and costs

Downloaded **7,897 actual historical option trade bars for 21 predetermined contracts**, alongside 8,190 regular-session SPY minute bars. Every selected contract returned data. The option history endpoint is [documented by Alpaca](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/v1.4.2/reference/optionbars). These are trade aggregates, not historical bid/ask quotes. No broker orders or live setting changes were made.

The baseline adds 3% adverse cost per side to the observed opening trade price, rounds adversely to a cent, and assumes $0.65 per contract per side. These are scenario assumptions, not verified executable fills or the actual account fee schedule. Entry quantity is calculated at the proxy fill price within the cash cap; live whole-contract order sizing and price gaps can behave differently.

## Scope and assessment

This tests a new synthetic opening-momentum CALL/PUT rule and all-in cash allocation. It is not a controlled sizing comparison with the earlier three-alert-per-day stock strategy, and not a month of real creator alerts. The parser and candle gate are production code; entry fills, premium exit logic and cash accounting are research simulation code. The production receiver, broker adapter, actual exit monitor, liquidity queues and five-second polling are not replayed here. Account/PDT/settled-cash eligibility is not enforced.

**Profitability grade for this scenario: F.** Large losses occurred early; larger allocation did not create a profitable signal. This is one reused historical month, not an out-of-sample estimate of future returns or a reason to change live risk limits.

Ten targeted tests verify exact 0DTE parsing, no lookahead in contract selection, fee reserves/cash limits, forced close, premium stop timing and adverse gaps, trailing chronology, missing-data failure, unaffordable contracts, gate rejection and crossed source levels. **112 focused checks passed**, including these cases and existing month, gate and signed-alert simulations.
